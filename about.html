About the map

What the map shows, and how it works.

The map shows mineral locations, active mining sites and drilling activity across Nigeria. It is made for investors, and for anyone who wants to see where Nigeria's minerals are.

1,070 sites · 37 places — 36 states and the FCT · 42 minerals

The three marks

Checked — the site was checked against records and reports.

Likely — the signs point to it, but it is not yet fully checked.

Worked out — worked out from nearby workings, veins, trends and geology.

Pins show the town, not the exact spot.

New sites keep being added. The map reads a live data file, so it grows without a new version.
