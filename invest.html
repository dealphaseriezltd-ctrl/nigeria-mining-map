Why mining in Nigeria

The case in plain facts.

Nigeria's ground holds many minerals. This map alone lists 42 of them, from gold and lithium to tin, iron ore, coal, lead-zinc, limestone and bitumen.

Lithium goes into batteries. Tin goes into electronics. Limestone goes into cement. Gold is held as a store of value.

The rules have changed

Mining is run under the Nigerian Minerals and Mining Act of 2007. The Mining Cadastre Office gives out titles through a digital system, first come, first served. Permits take up to 30 working days. Leases take up to 45. Licence fees were reviewed in July 2024. Titles follow a use-it-or-lose-it rule: hold ground and work it, or lose it.

Before you put money in

Check the licence status. Check the landowner consent. Read the geology reports. Talk to a licensed adviser.
